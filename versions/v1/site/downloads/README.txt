Microscope probe lift — build123d prototype 03

End mount for a Ø44 mm microscope cylinder, with a nominal 44.4 mm split-clamp bore and a 100 mm working distance to the specimen. The carriage moves 40 mm straight vertically; the externally held probe remains 60° above the plate. A custom necked metal probe has a 1 mm stem in the central field and a 6 mm shank in the external collet. Confirm the actual objective envelope and optical field.

Drive: module 1, 20° pressure angle, 20-tooth pinion engaging 60 internal teeth in a 180° slot of a virtual 120-tooth ring. Ratio 6:1. Wheel OD 136 mm; crank radius 25 mm; connecting rod 110 mm; wheel sweep approximately 106.26° for the 40 mm stroke.

Probe reference: custom 104 mm metal probe. The first 4 mm is pointed; the 1 mm stem continues to 60 mm from the tip; a taper spans 60–70 mm; the 6 mm shank spans 70–104 mm. Collet mid-plane is 90 mm from the tip, at world X=45 mm. This is an illustrative probe geometry, not a supplied or load-rated probe. Confirm suitability for your experiment.

Hardware: four 625ZZ bearings (5 × 16 × 5 mm). M5 structural and pivot fasteners; four M3 motor mounting screws. Modeled M5 heads are low profile, 8.5 mm diameter × 3.5 mm high. Use smooth precision 5 mm running sections through bearing races, with M5 threads outside the races. Standard 6 mm shoulder bolts do not fit. Verify actual screw lengths, head envelopes, motor mounting and shaft dimensions.

Nominal allowances: microscope bore +0.4 mm diameter; bearing seats +0.1 mm diameter; probe bore +0.35 mm diameter; M5 clearance holes 5.3 mm; dovetail lateral clearance 0.3 mm. Calibrate for your material and process. Do not clamp rotating bearing races with the surrounding printed shoulder.
