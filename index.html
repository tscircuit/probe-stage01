Designed around the microscope end. Split clamp, braced frame, NEMA17 drive, 625ZZ bearings and M5 pivots.

Geometry & fit

40 mm of straight vertical mechanical travel; the probe stays 60° above the specimen plate. Inspect the clearance indicator as you move.

The optical keepout projects the Ø44 mm barrel to the specimen. Confirm the actual optical field and objective envelope against your microscope.

CAD downloads

STEP files preserve solid geometry for each component. A genuine Parasolid exporter is unavailable, so x_t is unavailable. Import STEP into your CAD tool to export x_t.
